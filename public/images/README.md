# Images

- `profile.jpg` — your headshot. Square, at least 800×800 px. Picked up automatically by
  the `Avatar` component; until it exists, initials are shown instead.
- `og-cover.png` — 1200×630 px social share image referenced from `index.html`. Generated from
  `design/og-cover.html`; see `design/README.md` to regenerate after edits.
